# Thesis Sensitivity Map

The thesis sensitivity map prevents universal direction assumptions. It matches source type, event domain, event category, and impact channel to a thesis-specific direction, sensitivity, and affected assumption.

The same shipping disruption may map bullish for an oil producer and bearish for an airline. A rate cut may map bullish through liquidity or bearish through a recession channel. Reddit crowding may increase a volatility assumption without increasing bull probability.

Unknown or unmatched evidence creates no forced probability update.
