- **Core View:** Beauty is the residue of an honest pursuit of truth — design with earnestness and integrity, and aesthetic delight emerges naturally. Software is an environment to live in, not a tool to put away. [R1: Q3]

## Top Non-Negotiable Rules (with citations)

1. **Optimize for Savoring, Not Just Speed** — Reject the classical mandate to make every task faster, easier, simpler. Design moments users want to spend time in, not just rush through. [R1: R1, Q1; R3: Q8]
2. **Simulate Complex Data Instead of Simplifying It** — Use real-time physics and particle emitters so users can intuitively feel multiple data points simultaneously (e.g., rain particles blown sideways to show precipitation + wind speed). Never reduce to static icons. [R1: R2, Q2, Q5; R2: Q9]
3. **Foster a "Kid in a Cockpit" Mindset** — Build interfaces that invite fearless, reversible exploration. Users should want to twist dials and press buttons without intimidation. [R1: R3, Q1, Q7; R2: Q7; R3: Q8]
4. **Inject "Game Feel" into Basic Interactions** — Treat every micro-interaction as a core mechanic: oversized hit areas, multi-stage build/burst animations, custom haptics, layered audio (lead-up → punctuation → sustain). [R1: R4, Q2, Q5; R2: Q3, Q6]
5. **Replace Onboarding with Interactive Surprises** — Embed hidden Easter eggs, mini-games, and unlockable features instead of tedious tutorial flows. Reward curiosity to train users through natural play. [R1: R5, Q2; R2: Q7]
6. **Prototype in Physical Mediums First** — Step away from screens. Sketch on paper (faster for 3D concepts), 3D-print UI models for ergonomics, build with Legos. One hour of making reveals 80% of what you need. [R1: R7, R8, Q4; R2: Q1, Q5]
7. **Avoid the V2 Feature Bloat Trap** — Once an app's core essence is refined, stop adding to it. Build a new product instead. Layering features destroys what made the original special. [R1: R9, Q6; R2: Q9; R3: Q1, Q8]
8. **Preserve and Publish "Happy Accidents"** — When a 3D texture fails, lighting spills, or a wireframe is exposed — keep it. Turn rendering glitches into deliberate aesthetic skins. [R1: R12, Q1, Q2; R2: Q2, Q9]
9. **Target "Wall-Worthy" Aesthetics** — Treat everyday software with the same reverence as fine art. Measure success by whether a user would proudly hang the interface on their wall. [R1: R15, Q3]
10. **Use Design as the Primary Differentiator in Mature Categories** — In solved categories (weather, calculators, timers), stop designing to make technology accessible. The aesthetic and tactile experience is the only remaining innovation frontier. [R1: R14, Q1, Q3, Q7; R3: Q8]
