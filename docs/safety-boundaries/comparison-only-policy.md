# Research and recommendation boundary

Outputs describe differences under supplied assumptions. They must distinguish calculated results, modeled estimates, unavailable values, and user-selected exposure.

The system may calculate, model, forecast, compare, flag risks, and produce a model-implied recommendation under supplied assumptions and retrieved or verified evidence. The user chooses whether to accept, modify, reject, or record it.

The system does not execute a transaction, claim certainty, impersonate a connected account, or silently change assumptions.
