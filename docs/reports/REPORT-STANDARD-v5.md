Derived from the 9 October 2026 revision 5 reporting standard. This public adaptation removes personal report history, assignments, acceptance scores, local paths and schedule-specific details. It does not ship private reports. Exact source SHA-256: `38d640ca9166eaab8e47b0f0f3a257ef4026657ff2b06822c21b0dad957759c2`. Source revision/date and exact hash identify the supplied document; private storage identifiers are omitted.

## Current educational boundary

Provide research for the user's own decisions and predictions: business evidence, competing interpretations, hypothetical scenarios, dissent, triggers and uncertainty. Do not select personal transactions, weights, funding, borrowing or repayment actions. Replace prescriptive summaries and images themselves; a disclaimer alone is insufficient. Original historical engine/schema/card names do not override this policy. Public-company research remains useful without holdings.

User-set constraints may define hypothetical comparisons; label them, disclose omissions, and compare consequences without choosing an allocation. Do not infer objectives, risk tolerance, liquidity needs or acceptable position size.

## Four evidence classes

- Measured: source-reported observation with source, reference period, publication and retrieval cutoff. It is not independent assurance. Price quotes include timestamp, session, currency and delay/executability limits.
- Calculated: deterministic arithmetic from observed inputs; show input lineage, formulas, units and code identity. Correct arithmetic does not validate sources.
- Modeled: assumption-dependent results including valuation, probability scenarios, forward returns, scenario Sharpe, Kelly and stress tests. Use “Modeled · calculated in Python” when applicable. Precision does not turn assumptions into observations.
- Forecasted: explicit future claim with author/source, issue date, horizon and uncertainty. Guidance is “Forecasted · company-provided”. Do not invent predictions to fill this category.

Screenshots are Observed · unconfirmed until actual confirmation. A market contract price is a Measured quote; its future expectation is Forecasted by participants and is affected by rules, fees, liquidity and rounding. It is not automatically a calibrated analyst probability or a stock-return probability. Calendar entries are announced timing, not guarantees.

## Lineage, metric correctness and computation

Preserve each input's identifier, raw value, currency/unit, source/artifact, reference/publication/retrieval timestamps, adjustment convention, confidence and observed/transcribed/revised/assumed status. For every output retain input IDs, formula/version, units, horizon, code/module hash, executed command, raw output, seeds and exact assumptions. Round only for display; explain mismatches rather than invent reconciliation.

Match return periods, annualization, risk-free units, currencies and price-adjustment conventions. Do not treat scenario dispersion as historical volatility, historical Sharpe as forward certainty, or differently conditioned hypothetical models as comparable stock rankings. Kelly remains an assumption-bound mathematical reference, never executable sizing. Borrowing/maintenance stresses are hypothetical and not brokerage confirmation. Run deterministic calculations in Python with bounded work; independent checks must use valid domains and units.

Each report lens needs a distinct question, evidence assessment, rule, substantive dissent, missing evidence/abstention and research consequence. Commentary is simulated, never a real attributed quotation or endorsement. A requested single lens does not silently prove all frameworks were applied. Page is a separate many-model robustness note, not a ninth vote or claimed calibrated ensemble probability.

Map all thirteen analytical areas from the bundled anatomy: optional position context; Porter/Helmer/BMC; Bayesian update; risk/Sharpe/Kelly; engines; convexity; seeded simulation; margin; options; barbell comparisons; eight lenses; metrology; research receipt. Missing areas are explicit, not silently counted complete. Preserve evidence sidebar and source links. Formal Bayesian updates require actual verified evidence plus matching sensitivity rules.

## HTML-first reading and design

Use the company's common name as the masthead; ticker is metadata. HTML is primary; print/PDF is optional and separately verified. Retain warm paper, editorial hierarchy, restrained signal color, readable type, hairline rules, square geometry and relevant chart labels without imitating a publication's name/logo. Choose and preserve an appropriate supplied design family. Report source/audit navigation precedes strategy and final image.

The reading sequence covers research overview, evidence cutoff, business/competitive evidence, measured observations, calculated fundamentals, modeled alternatives, sourced forecasts if any, eight frameworks and separate Page, uncertainty/triggers, audit, and final visual synthesis. A complete business-model section includes all nine BMC blocks and all seven Helmer Powers with evidence, counterevidence and missing information. A blank heading is not substantive completion.

## Purposeful reversible exploration

Interactive views may reveal already computed scenario results and exact frozen strings. Preserve each model's original assumptions, horizon, probabilities and caps. Controls must show which scenario is selected, support keyboard/reset, and work with usable static tables and charts when JavaScript is absent. Never invent time series, scores, radar dimensions or geometry merely for visual variety. Presentations must not silently recalculate financial outputs in JavaScript.

## Final generated sketchnote

For full acceptance, generate one actual PNG through one named simulated investor framework using Dan Roam's 6×5 visual thinking. Preserve a complete text equivalent, alt text, exact PNG hash, intrinsic dimensions, prompt/host receipt and actual pixel-inspection receipt. An SVG, placeholder or synthetic test PNG is not a completed generated sketchnote. It must match verified source strings and model assumptions and contain no prescriptive personal portfolio conclusion.

Record lens rotation separately in authorized private state. Advance only after a successfully saved/delivered accepted revision; a failed generation or edit preserving the prior approved image does not advance rotation. No private historical rotation assignments are bundled. Preserve source inputs, original financial data and earlier reports/audits when repairing presentation; do not claim a financial refresh without actual new evidence.
