# Measured, Calculated, Modeled, and Forecast

`measured` identifies observed or user-verified source evidence at a time. It does not mean universally true.

`calculated` identifies deterministic math from referenced inputs.

`modeled` identifies an inference from evidence, assumptions, and explicit rules.

`forecast` identifies a forward-looking estimate under supplied assumptions, verified evidence, and model rules. It does not mean guaranteed.

Every semantic output is an evidence atom with a value, provenance class, and applicable unit, input references, formula reference, and limitations.
