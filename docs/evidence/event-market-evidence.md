# Event-Market Evidence

Event-market snapshots record measured title, outcome probabilities, volume, resolution rules, capture time, and verification status. Calculated outputs include consensus, surprise probability, top-two gap, normalized entropy, event volatility, and conservative volume confidence.

Domain metrics cover rates, geopolitics, technology, commodities, market structure, and company-specific events. These readings do not carry a universal asset direction. A separate thesis sensitivity rule determines whether an event is bullish, bearish, mixed, volatility-increasing, neutral, or unknown for a particular thesis.

Only `user_verified` and `user_corrected` snapshots are eligible for belief updates.
