# No model-created facts

The system or model may retrieve, explain, compare, format, and label information supplied by the user, observed through a provenance-complete connector, or produced by deterministic calculation.

It may not invent financial facts, probabilities, prices, account balances, market data, tax assumptions, expected returns, user decisions, or authorizations. Retrieved observations must retain their source, time, raw hash, method, confidence, status, and limitations. Missing inputs remain unavailable and are labeled accordingly.
